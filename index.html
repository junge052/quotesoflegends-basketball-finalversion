    About

    Every basketball game ends twice. The first ending comes with the buzzer: the final score, the handshakes, the confetti or the quiet walk down the tunnel. The second comes minutes later, in a cramped room with a curtained backdrop and a cluster of microphones, where a player fresh off the court has to explain what just happened.

    That second ending is where this site lives. The quotes here span 1970 to 2026, from the era of notepad-and-pencil beat writers to a modern game where every sentence is clipped and shared within minutes. They were gathered from transcripts of interview clips and from basketball coverage, including news outlets and the NBA’s own channels.

    You’ll find humility and bravado, grace in defeat and, occasionally, a spectacular lack of it. You’ll find the familiar liturgy of “just trying to help the team win,” and the moments when someone tosses it aside and simply says what they mean.
